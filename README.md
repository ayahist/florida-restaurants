# What Happens After Someone Reports a Restaurant in Florida?

## Project overview

This project analyzes more than 681,000 Florida restaurant inspections to understand what happens after a complaint is filed. It compares complaint inspections with routine inspections to see whether complaints uncover more violations and whether they are more likely to result in enforcement actions.

## Findings

- Complaint inspections uncovered more violations on average than routine inspections.
- They also uncovered more high-priority violations.
- Complaint inspections were more likely to find three or more high-priority violations.
- Despite that, nearly half of complaint inspections resulted in no further action.

## Data

The data comes from the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) restaurant inspection records.

(Data source link)

## Data collection

The inspection records were downloaded from the Florida DBPR and cleaned using Python and pandas before being analyzed.

## Analysis

The analysis compared:

- Routine vs. complaint inspections
- Average total violations
- Average high-priority violations
- Inspections with three or more high-priority violations
- Complaint inspection outcomes

Charts were created using Datawrapper.
